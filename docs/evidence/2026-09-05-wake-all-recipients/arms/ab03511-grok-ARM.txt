## 4. `parseSignalRecipients` — PASS

Absent vs `[]`: `undefined` → `{}` (no key). `[]` → `{ recipients: [] }`. `signalReadJsonPayload` passes the parsed records through. `JSON.stringify` drops a missing key and keeps `"recipients":[]`.
