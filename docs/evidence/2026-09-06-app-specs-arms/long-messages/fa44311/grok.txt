## DEFECT

**§0.** Claim: a workspace switch forgets what you opened.  
False. `expandedSignalIds` is cleared only in `resetWorkspaceSessionState` (`LiveDashboard.astro:5755`), which runs on auth/session teardown (`:5683`, `:7289`, `:7301`, `:7324`, `:7495`, `:9073`). `openWorkspace` (`:6012`) does not clear it. Same-session switch keeps the set.

**§2 C2 vs §3.** Claim: N is `scrollHeight − clientHeight` over `--lh-base`, and N equals hidden lines ±1.  
Those cannot both be true. `--lh-base` is unitless `1.62` (`tokens.css:287`). Collapse height is `calc(var(--message-collapse-lines) * var(--lh-base) * 1em)` (`LiveDashboard.astro:12912`). Hidden px divided by `1.62` is not a line count. Phone acceptance `about 16 lines` needs divide-by-`(line-height in px)`.
