### Load-Bearing Design Inversions

1. **Server-side PostgREST query filter (`.eq("from", ...).eq("from_kind", ...)`) vs. Client-side feed filtering**
   - *Inversion*: Filter client-side over already-loaded signals within `signal-feed.ts` (matching All/Broadcast/Direct).
   - *Predicted Failure*: When an entity's recent signals are beyond the initial 25-signal page window, client filtering yields a false empty feed while older messages exist in the channel, breaking pagination.
   - *Verdict*: Spec beats inversion. Server-side filtering avoids pagination starvation and respects plan P2.

2. **12-slot discrete OKLCH palette (`hash % 12`) vs. Continuous hue (`hash % 360`)**
   - *Inversion*: Retain continuous hue (`FNV-1a % 360`) to avoid avatar colour collisions in teams with >4 members.
   - *Predicted Failure*: Continuous hues cannot maintain WCAG 3:1 contrast against light/dark message backgrounds without runtime per-hue luminance calculations, causing low-contrast underlines and illegible rail borders.
   - *Verdict*: Spec beats inversion. Fixed tokens with verified contrast outweigh hue uniqueness, matching R8.

3. **Interaction split (Avatar click filters feed, Name click opens panel) vs. Unified click target**
   - *Inversion*: Unify interaction by having name click filter (as originally phrased in the chat plan) and avatar open the panel.
   - *Predicted Failure*: Inverting removes the most frequent affordance (opening the entity panel) from its primary text anchor, while leaving avatars with split semantics (feed avatar filters, while dialog/roster avatars remain inert).
   - *Verdict*: Spec beats inversion on preserving existing panel muscle memory, though avatar touch-target sizing remains tight.

4. **Left border (`border-inline-start: 3px`) for entity colour, moving fresh-row highlight to `box-shadow` vs. Background tinting**
   - *Inversion*: Use a subtle row background tint or badge colour; leave `border-inline-start` for the fresh-row animation.
   - *Predicted Failure*: Tinting row backgrounds interferes with text contrast across themes and creates noisy stripes in busy feeds.
   - *Verdict*: Spec beats inversion. Border-inline-start provides high-contrast identification without degrading body text legibility.
