### 1. The header rule
I checked whether the channel head and filter row are out of flow. The head uses `position: absolute` (line 1528 of `DIFF.patch`, `site/src/components/app/LiveDashboard.astro`), and the filter row neutralizes its flow height using `margin-block-end: -2.5rem` against a `min-block-size: 2.5rem` (lines 1584-1585).
