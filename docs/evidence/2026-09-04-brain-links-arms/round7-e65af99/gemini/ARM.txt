1. **Trace it:**
   A control CAN render for a deleted topic because rendering uses a snapshot of the workspace files. However, it CANNOT open a topic the workspace does not hold.
   - **`createBrainTopicReader`'s serialization**: If a click happens while a cadence read is in flight, the forced refresh waits for it to finish and starts a NEW read. This guarantees the list is fresh *as of the click*.
   - **`listIsFresh`**: If this forced read fails or bails, `listIsFresh` is false. `brainLinkClickOutcome` then refuses to open the topic, returning `missing` with a connection message.
   - **`contextIsCurrent`**: The click captures `requestVersion` and `activeWorkspaceId` before the await. If the workspace switches during the read, `contextIsCurrent` becomes false. `brainLinkClickOutcome` checks this *first* and returns `abandoned`, doing nothing.
   - **Switch during `brainView.open`**: The `contextIsCurrent` check and the `brainView?.open()` call are synchronous. No workspace switch can interleave between them.
