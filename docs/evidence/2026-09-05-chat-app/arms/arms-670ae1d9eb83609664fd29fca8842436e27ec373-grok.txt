### 3. `postedSinceReset` holds the row — and that is where this SHA breaks

The Map, `rowShowsOnScreen` as one rule, and `forgetFetchedPostedIds` on every landed page close the Gemini id-only hole **inside one workspace**:

- Stay in the channel: success writes the row into the Map; a lagging reset page puts it back.
- Switch to all-signals after success: `rowShowsOnScreen` is true; the row belongs there.
- Switch to another named channel: `rowShowsOnScreen` is false; the row is not painted there; coming back still has the row in the Map.
- `signals = []` on a move no longer destroys the only copy.

Session reset clears the Map (`:4852`). Sample mode never reads it.

What the Map fix did **not** do: scope the Map to a workspace. `openWorkspace` clears `signals`, `channels`, `activeChannelId`, and `unknownChannelId` (`:5205–5216`). It calls `resetComposer()` on a real workspace change (`:5171`). Neither path assigns `postedSinceReset`. `rowShowsOnScreen` (`:1355–1357`) does not know a workspace. `Signal` has no `workspaceId`. The merge (`:4632–4636`) now reads the Map, not `signals`.

That is finding 1.
