1. **Copy that implies privacy.**
- **Finding:** `site/src/components/app/LiveDashboard.astro`, lines 4138 (in `renderUnknownChannel`) and 5528 (in `renderChannelHead`).
- **What breaks:** Both locations include the string `"This link names a channel that is not in this workspace, or one you cannot read..."`. The phrase "one you cannot read" directly contradicts the design rule that every workspace member reads every channel, by suggesting to the reader that there are restricted channels they are not authorized to see.
- **How you would see it:** Navigating to a URL with a `?c=` channel ID that does not exist in the workspace displays this message.
- **Why the sweep test missed it:** The regex in `chat-channels.observer.test.ts` checks for explicit substrings like `private`, `secret`, `encrypted`, or variations of `only...see/read`, but does not match "cannot read".
- **Refutation check:** Could "one you cannot read" refer to a channel in a different workspace? The preceding clause "not in this workspace" already covers that scenario. Therefore, "or one you cannot read" distinctly implies unreadable channels within the current workspace.
