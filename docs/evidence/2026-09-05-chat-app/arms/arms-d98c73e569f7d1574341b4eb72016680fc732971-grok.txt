1. **`POSTED_ROW_GRACE_MS` bounds lifetime, not position. A row that has fallen off the first page can still be painted as the newest message.**
   - **File**: `site/src/components/app/LiveDashboard.astro`
   - **Line**: 4664–4670 (`prunePostedRows` then `[...arrivedWhileFetching, ...page.rows]`), with `SIGNAL_PAGE_SIZE = 25` at `:1510` and the poll at `:4996` (`2_000`).
   - **What breaks**: Prune only asks *age* and *workspace*. The merge already sorts stand-ins by `createdAt` among themselves (`:4668`), then still puts that whole block **in front of** `page.rows`. Replica lag of a post this browser just wrote is the one case that needs that: the stand-in is newer than `page.rows[0]`. Page-roll is a different case with the same bits: the replica **has** the row, it is just not on the first 25, `fetched.has(row.id)` is false, `rowShowsOnScreen` is true, and the row is prepended as if it were newest. Thirty seconds vs a two-second poll is a replica-lag bound. It is not a page-roll bound. Filling 25 rows in 30 seconds is 0.83 messages per second. This product’s agents emit signals as they work; all-signals is the whole feed, so it fills faster than a named channel.
   - **How you see it**: In `#all-signals` (or `#mobile`), post “hello”. Click another place at once, before a page of the first place carries the id. Wait until 25 newer messages land in that place, still inside 30 seconds. Click back. “hello” sits at the top above messages that are newer than it. The next poll (~2s) does `[...page.rows, ...signals.filter(not on page)]` and moves it under the first page, where it then sticks as an extra row until prune runs on a later reset.
   - **Refutation**: Does 30s stop the *days-later* Gemini loop? Yes — prune deletes the row on the next reset after the grace, so it cannot return tomorrow and it cannot grow for the life of the tab. Does `forgetFetchedPostedIds` save it if they never leave? Yes — the first poll that carries the id drops it; this needs the leave-before-that-page path Gemini already named. Could replica lag and page-roll be told apart without a new invented comparison? Yes: both timestamps are the server’s. A lagging new post has `createdAt >= page.rows[0].createdAt` (or the page is empty) and should stand in. A rolled-off row has `createdAt < page.rows[0].createdAt` on a full page and should not. The 30s hammer does not ask that question. Nothing else in this lane reads `POSTED_ROW_GRACE_MS`; the observer pins the literal and the prune call, not “do not prepend an older row”. Gates stay green.
