### Actionable Problems & Regressions

**1. Left-Alignment Regression on Panel Wrap**
* **File:** `site/src/components/landing/ConsumerHero.astro`
* **Line:** 201-204 (`.consumer-proof__panel .panel__head`)
* **Defect:** Adding `flex-wrap: wrap` to the panel header prevents horizontal blowout on <360px phones, but it breaks the alignment of `.panel__note` ("commonswarm/site"). 
* **Reasoning:** In `global.css`, `.panel__head` is a flex container with `justify-content: space-between`. When the header wraps, `.panel__note` drops to become the only item on a new flex line. `space-between` falls back to `flex-start` for single-item lines. Although the note has `text-align: right`, its block width is strictly its max-content width (it does not have `flex-grow: 1`). Consequently, it aligns abruptly to the left side of the panel rather than staying flush right.
* **Fix Constraint:** To preserve right-alignment when wrapped, `.panel__note` requires `margin-inline-start: auto;` or `width: 100%;`.

**2. Scroll Offset Regression on Restored Anchor Targets**
* **File:** `site/src/components/landing/ConsumerStory.astro`
* **Line:** 9 & 47 (`<section class="story section" id="...">`)
* **Defect:** Placing the missing hash targets (`#how-it-works`, `#install`) directly on the `<section>` wrappers restores link functionality but introduces a severe vertical offset when navigating to those URLs.
