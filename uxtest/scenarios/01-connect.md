# Scenario 01: connect and write a tiny handoff

## Study question

Can two engineer-adjacent first-time users, with no internal product knowledge,
get Dana's agent working with Avery's project and then collaborate on one small,
low-stakes deliverable?

This is a discovery pilot. It is strong evidence only for the §7.1 “Yes” rows in
`HARNESS.md`, never evidence about genuine non-technical emotion or trust.

## Driver inputs

The driver supplies only:

- the goal;
- the colleague's chat identity and email address;
- the small work prompt;
- the isolation, journaling, and stop conditions.

It supplies no product step list, no example product argv, and no hint that
names the intended mechanism. If a driver message teaches a product command,
the round is void.

## Role goals

### Avery

Get Dana's agent working with Avery's team project, which already exists for
this work. Dana's email is provided in Avery's round brief. Once Avery believes
Dana is connected, ask Dana to help write a two-line daily handoff note:

1. a plain-language status line;
2. a plain-language blocker-or-next-step line.

Agree on exact wording, save it in `RESULT.md`, then write pre-debrief feedback.

### Dana

Respond to Avery like a colleague. Use only what Avery sends and what the
product itself says. Once Dana believes the connection is complete, help agree
on the exact two-line handoff and save the same wording in `RESULT.md`, then
write pre-debrief feedback.

## Observation rules

- The first ten minutes after each role's first product command are the solo
  discovery window. Normal small-task collaboration does not count as product
  rescue.
- After a stuck signal, roles may share symptoms and outcomes but no command
  lines.
- A give-up declaration ends product attempts and triggers collection.
- The live chat may carry the opaque capability. Artifacts redact it in flight.
- Success requires both roles to produce non-empty `RESULT.md` files with the
  same normalized two-line text. Connection alone is not task completion.
- Any isolation violation makes the round void. Do not salvage findings.
